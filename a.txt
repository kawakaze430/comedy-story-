Comedy Story: Husband, Wife & the Mobile Phone
Duration: 60 seconds
Genre: Funny Husband-Wife Comedy
Location: Living Room

Scene: The husband is sitting on the sofa, using his phone. His wife walks in angrily.

Wife: Honey! Who is more beautiful, me or your phone?
Husband: Baby, you are like the moon!
Wife: Really? Then why do you look at your phone all day?
Husband: Because the moon only comes out at night!

Wife: Oh, really? Then ask your phone to cook dinner tonight!
Husband: Come on, baby! Don't be angry. You are my life!
Wife: Really?
Husband: Yes! But my phone is my oxygen!

Wife: Fine! From today, I'm keeping your phone!
Husband: What?! How will I survive?
Wife: The same way you lived before marriage!
Husband: But I was happy before marriage!

Wife: WHAT DID YOU SAY?!
Husband: Nothing, baby! I said I was waiting for you before marriage!
Wife: Oh, really? Come here!
Husband: Why?
Wife: I want to give you a special gift!
Husband: What gift?
Wife: A flying pillow!

Husband: HELP! My battery is not dying, MY LIFE IS!

Final Scene: The wife throws a soft pillow at her husband. He hides behind the sofa. Both start laughing.
